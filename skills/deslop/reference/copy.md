# Interface copy without the AI voice

Write in the project's language (`profile.md`), speaking to the person. The pattern-level copy signals (em dash abuse, forced contrast, hype claims, prompt leakage) are in section 7 and 10 of `catalog.md`.

## Voice

- Verb first when there is an action.
- Short sentences, no jargon, no full stop in labels and titles.
- The exact data instead of the adjective ("dries out Nov 2–4", not "dries out soon").
- Advice without the AI voice: state the cause as fact and the concrete action. No hedged filler, and no coloured box around the advice.

## Tells are per language

AI filler is specific to each language. Check the list for the language the product is written in. Do not translate one list into the other: a phrase that is a tell in one language is often natural, or nonexistent, in the other. For a language not listed here, ask the user for three phrases that sound machine-written to them and record them in `taste.md`.

### English

- Hedged advice: "worth checking", "you may want to", "it might be a good idea to", "tends to", "keep an eye on".
- Hype words: "Elevate", "Seamless", "Next-generation", "Supercharge", "Unleash", "Empower", "world-class", "effortless".
- Forced contrast: "It's not a feature. It's a platform."
- Dismissal by label: calling something "theater" instead of saying what is ineffective and why.
- Greeting as landing page: "Welcome to your Dashboard, [Name] ✨" with a grey subtitle promising to "experience the power of…".

### Portuguese (Brazil)

- Conselho com rodeio: "pede atenção", "vale conferir", "costuma ser", "pode ser interessante", "fique de olho".
- Palavras de hype: "eleve", "potencialize", "revolucione", "de forma simples e intuitiva", "na palma da sua mão", "descomplicado".
- Contraste forçado: "Não é um recurso. É uma plataforma."
- Saudação de landing dentro de ferramenta: "Bem-vindo ao seu Painel, [Nome] ✨" com subtítulo cinza prometendo "experimente o poder de…".
- Explicação óbvia da origem do dado: "você contou", "com base nos seus dados".
